Custom design

If you can imagine it, we can make it.

Our story

Family-owned. Award-winning. Making jewelry since 1965.

It started with a single pair of earrings. Mark and his parents designed and manufactured fine jewelry in their Hollywood, Florida workshop for some of the best stores in America, and by 1994 Mark's designs had won 10 De Beers Design Awards.

But something was missing: the moment a client puts the piece on. So the family brought their craft to Plantation, where you can work directly with the people who make your jewelry.

“Jewelry doesn't come to life until someone puts it on.”Mark, master jeweler
